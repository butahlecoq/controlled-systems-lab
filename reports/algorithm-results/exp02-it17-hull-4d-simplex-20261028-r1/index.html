EXP-02: first 4D hull-ladder cell

The workload has five 4-simplex corners and 245 dyadic interior points. Halton bases 2, 3, 5, and 7 deterministically select strictly positive barycentric weights in multiples of 1/8, making interior-point removal certificates exactly representable. Five LP reducers ran with one warmup and five measured calls each. An independent exact-rational validator verifies each removal certificate and its acyclic expansion to the retained source points. This proves equality of the finite input hull and returned hull even when a reducer conservatively retains some redundant points.

QuickHull is recorded as unsupported because the registered geometric implementation is 2D-only. The six plotted projections show input points only; red markers identify the 4-simplex corners.
