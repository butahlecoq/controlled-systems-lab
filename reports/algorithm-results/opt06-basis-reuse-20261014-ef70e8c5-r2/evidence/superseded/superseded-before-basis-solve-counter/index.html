Hardware/compiler/build details and all raw rows are retained in records.json, trials.csv, summary.csv, raw stdout JSONL, raw stderr, and the sanitized build log. LP/pivot/bulk counters describe operations, not elapsed-time components.

The fixtures are intentionally small and do not model the fuel-consumption application or establish production workload scaling. Timed methods are alternated, but no tuned performance verdict is drawn; a dedicated limiter profile and broader workload family would be needed before claiming a winner.
