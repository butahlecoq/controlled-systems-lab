The current Qhull polar-facet adapter is not claimed to port either source. Executable equivalence is not established. See docs/contracts/polyhedral-conversion.md#lineage-audit-a17-and-a18.

Limitations

  • Only 2D square, simplex, embedded segment and singleton contract fixtures are measured; all have at most four vertices.
  • Ray-bearing V inputs, unbounded sets, empty sets, higher-dimensional sets and large facet counts are unsupported by the selected bounded-only adapter and were excluded from timing.
  • No comparison adapter is available in this report; no speedup or rank claim is made.
  • The selected implementation uses binary64 reductions, LP prerequisite checks and Qhull 8.0.2. Support/equivalence evidence remains numerical and non-certified.
  • No conversion decomposition into LP, affine reduction and Qhull substage timings is exposed. ETW was resolved to modules but not functions, so it cannot identify a per-stage algorithmic limiter.
  • The A17/A18 audit is limited to archived source text and inventory digests; it does not establish executable equivalence or historical numerical guarantees.
