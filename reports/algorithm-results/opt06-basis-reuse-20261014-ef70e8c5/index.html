Evidence and limits

Every warmup and timed pair returns complete reductions. For each timed pair, the harness retains the exact outputs from the timed invocations and, after timing stops, checks both hull containments using fresh registered HiGHS LP membership queries. This includes the interior-basis fixture: its baseline is complete, and the basis-reuse result reports structural basis IDs [0, 3, 4] and retains center ID 0. The focused unit test independently asserts the completed baseline, basis membership of ID 0, and both containments.

Hardware/compiler/build details and all raw rows are retained in records.json, trials.csv, summary.csv, raw stdout JSONL, raw stderr, and the sanitized build log. LP/pivot/bulk counters describe operations, not elapsed-time components.

The fixtures are intentionally small and do not model the fuel-consumption application or establish production workload scaling. Timed methods are alternated, but no tuned performance verdict is drawn; a dedicated limiter profile and broader workload family would be needed before claiming a winner.
