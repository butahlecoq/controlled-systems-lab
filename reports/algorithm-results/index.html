This is the published archive of measured algorithm results. Every entry names the algorithm family, explains in plain language what the report measures, repeats the scope and the evidence limits recorded with the report, and links to the report page, its raw records, its measured tables and its offline bundle.

31 published reports · versioned and reproducible · numerical observations, not mathematical certificates

Read the evidence, not just the numbers. Measurements do not imply cross-method performance, application-scale results or mathematical certification. Each report states its own measured scope and its own limits; open a report before drawing a conclusion from it.
