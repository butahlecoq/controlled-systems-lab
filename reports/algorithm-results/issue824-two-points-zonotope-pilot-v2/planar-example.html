A planar linear image enclosed by zonotope propagation

This supported two-state example starts with the square [-1,1] × [-1,1], applies one stage x⁺ = diag(2,1)x, with zero control and zero disturbance, and uses universal safe sets. The output set is the axis-aligned rectangle [-2,2] × [-1,1].

Checked result: the independently reconstructed four exact image vertices are [[-2.0, -1.0], [2.0, -1.0], [2.0, 1.0], [-2.0, 1.0]]. The saved propagation trace has center [0.0, 0.0] and outward-rounded coordinate generator radii at least [2,1]. Its axis-aligned generators form an outer rectangle containing all exact image vertices. This inclusion check applies to this simple planar fixture; the four-state TwoPoints trace has only coordinate-bound checks and no full-dimensional membership proof.
