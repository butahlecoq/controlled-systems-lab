TwoPointsOnParallelLines: outer-set candidate selection

This is a three-stage finite-catalog adaptation of the source differential-game environment. The zonotope and fixed-frame parallelotope selectors rank every fixed open-loop plan by terminal enclosure-corner scores, then replay their selected plans through every finite disturbance history. These numerical candidate scores are not validated bounds. The report does not reproduce the upstream continuous-action RL problem.

Evidence and limits

The independent checker reconstructs the exact finite game, verifies all 27 zonotope and parallelotope candidate rows by evaluating their stored terminal corners, checks every finite-history state against all exact facet inequalities of each dyadic zonotope at every stage, checks both selected plans' exact worst disturbance histories, and transforms every finite-history state into the fixed parallelotope frame at each stage. The parallelotope orientation is fixed and is not optimized. Candidate scores are not validated objective bounds. The separate two-dimensional example checks complete inclusion of all finite terminal points in each axis-aligned zonotope.

The figure shows exact forward reachable sets, robust backward-winning states, their intersections, the box-selected and zonotope-selected outer set projections, the parallelotope selector's chosen outer set, and exact replay paths in both player phase planes. The fixed-plan policies use finite catalogs only; they make no continuous feedback optimality claim. The zonotope calls evaluate 884,736 factor corners each (27 plans × 32,768 corners); parallelotope scoring evaluates 432 corners in total (27 plans × 16). `perf` and `strace` were unavailable, so these are known work counts, not profiler-confirmed CPU bottlenecks.
