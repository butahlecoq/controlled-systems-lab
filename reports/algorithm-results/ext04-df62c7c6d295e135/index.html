EXT-04 observed policy replay

Fixed-source, binary64 sampled trajectories through the public CSL::controllers replay API. The estimator and controller are caller-defined callbacks. These examples check one supplied initial state and error sequence each; they do not establish policy nonanticipativity, state-estimate accuracy, all-initial-states feasibility, all-disturbance performance, optimality, family completeness or validated rounding enclosure.
