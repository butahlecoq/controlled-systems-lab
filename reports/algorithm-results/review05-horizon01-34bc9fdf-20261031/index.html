Analytic fixture

x[t+1] = x[t] + u[t], x[0] = 0, 0 <= u[t] <= 1; minimize x[T].

Because x[T] = sum(u[t]), the exact optimal value is 0, attained by the all-zero history. The checker verifies required initial, transition, and control-bound rows and the zero witness where a relation is exposed. It does not claim that a returned candidate relation is the complete feasible or optimal family.
