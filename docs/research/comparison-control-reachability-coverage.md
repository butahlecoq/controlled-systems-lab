## Exact comparison-readiness gaps

1. **Old-side execution:** archive inventory includes L26 Python 2 and MATLAB listings A01/A03/A35-A38, but no comparable modernized/isolated legacy driver with canonical inputs, reproducible environment, output normalization and source hashes is established. Issue #2 explicitly reports standalone `OpenLoopControl.py`, full MATLAB/Pascal projects, exact arrays/seeds and some control histories absent. The existence of old listings is not a runnable comparator.
2. **B02:** L05 reports nonlinear/linearized reachability examples at dimensions 2 and 4 (horizons 60 and 8), but the source-to-experiment audit finds L26's active T=16 fuel demo does not match either example or implement the paper's distinct modified selection. Its historical generator/input bundle and approximation semantics remain missing. Thus the recorded B02 numbers cannot yet anchor a faithful old-vs-new run (`baselines.md` B02; `reachability-source-correspondence.md`).
3. **B04:** do not use the reported 30.899 as an acceptance value for the displayed model: exact invariant gives terminal-plane lower bound 160.880653946, inconsistent with that result. Canonical matrices/initial data, U rows, first-action convention and stage bounds are unresolved; issue #10 is open. Later saved witness pairs are a distinct recovered variant, not a canonical T=16 reproduction (`b04-restoration.md`, `b04-saved-witnesses.md`).
4. **B05:** published data compare adaptive and open-loop behavior, but policy labels/inputs need source reconstruction; the old adaptive driver is not a modern causal-optimal controller. Its report cannot be conflated with a single solve's runtime (`baselines.md` B05 and issue #5).
5. **State-constraint variants:** modern model contracts express stage constraints, but a comparison fixture must state whether constraints apply to x0…xT, whether strictness/nonemptiness is checked, and whether the output is a reachable set, terminal preimage, or feasible optimal family. No published T-S01/T-S02 result tests these historical state-constrained cases; B07 changes to robust disturbances and a finite action catalogue, so is not a drop-in substitute.
6. **First-release dependencies:** implemented Strategy A/B paths and verification are available on narrow T-S01/T-S02; the framework page documents a runnable paired example and a T-S01 paired record. Broader family completeness, selector admission, proof-bearing paired artifact serialization/replay, generalized task-to-report composition, and historical fuel restoration remain outside that release. T-S01's record supports only the two strategies on its exact two-step scalar integrator; it does not support reachability-set geometry or B02/B04/B05 comparisons.
7. **What reports support now:** the T-S01 paired bundle supports a descriptive, inconclusive seven-pair measurement of the exact registered fixture; standalone T-S01/T-S02 reports are per-strategy/profile evidence. None supports general performance rankings, scaling, historical reproduction, state-constrained polytope comparisons, or proof payload persistence (`syn-ts01-paired.../README.md:3-11`).

## Scope challenges / facts that qualify the plan

- The plan includes nominal forward reachability, but that does not imply fidelity to B02's nonlinear inclusion claims; the source correspondence specifically finds no such implication from L26.
- “With and without state constraints” spans materially distinct model and quantifier semantics. B07's robust finite-action safety result is not simply a constrained version of nominal reachability.
- A Strategy A vs Strategy B report on T-S01 is not evidence that reachability and direct joint optimization agree or scale on a strict-polytope, constrained fuel task.
- B04's displayed problem cannot serve as a validated numerical target until #10 resolves its model/result contradiction. Existing later witness evidence does not supersede the historical identity.
