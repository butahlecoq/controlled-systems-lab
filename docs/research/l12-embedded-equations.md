# L12 embedded-equation provenance: bounded B04 check

Date: 2026-10-16. This is a source-reading follow-up for [issue #2](https://github.com/butahlecoq/controlled-systems-lab/issues/2), not a reconstruction of the publication-generating run. The private editable draft L12 (`papers/ШориковАФ, КалевВИ, текст.doc`, 393,216 bytes) matched its [inventory](inventory.json) SHA-256 `3f92143b504b1d0d0167cfc370d4a4b30d5ef2b71315ae25b9775378fcefa4a0` before inspection. Its original bytes were read only and are not redistributed. Page numbers below are one-based pages in a local PDF rendering of that exact DOC, not inferred from the incomplete piece-table text extraction.

## How the equations were checked

The DOC compound file contains 118 `ObjectPool/_<id>/Equation Native` streams. LibreOffice 26.8.1.1, extracted to a temporary directory without installation, rendered the DOC to a 17-page PDF and converted a separate temporary copy to DOCX. A limited [Equation Editor 3.0 parser](https://github.com/Mengfei-Li/document-equation-migration/tree/4024fe5a419b0f35971b57228e00dba163cfc898) decoded the 94 version-3 streams into structural MathML. The other 24 version-5 streams survived the temporary DOCX conversion with byte-identical `Equation Native` content, allowing their original `ObjectPool` IDs to be matched by stream SHA-256; an exploratory [MTEF parser](https://github.com/AndyQsmart/MTEF-py/tree/c1bb6578fbbc25a1177b3e352cd643fca7b5b78a) supplied a secondary reading for selected version-5 streams. The final claims below come from visual inspection of the rendered equations against L11 (SHA-256 `e04c2f1609e2580c8878ab7fb91bc41151ccc8e0cf1f5deac4cef9fd860ce147`), A41 (SHA-256 `db1f29e55e9e3558b4f9eb4bd52c86931f210233d746a986610dab08102b3891`) and the [publisher's R40 PDF](https://sciencejournals.ru/issues/teorsist/2019/vol_2019/iss_1/TeorSist1902009Kalev/TeorSist1902009Kalev.pdf) (downloaded SHA-256 `d1e05b276de16e202b147cf73079b95d9fb4e0ae6df97bace8357a4168431ca8`). Neither converter is treated as a semantic authority for unreviewed equations. The temporary derivatives and original streams are not committed.

The weighted five-term terminal criterion on L12 p. 13 was checked visually against L11 p. 13, A41 p. 12 and [R40 p. 172](https://sciencejournals.ru/issues/teorsist/2019/vol_2019/iss_1/TeorSist1902009Kalev/TeorSist1902009Kalev.pdf): the coefficients are `64/9` on the third and fourth squared terms, and the fifth is `(x₂−8x₄/3)²`, all under one square root. A unique original `Equation Native` object for that complete displayed expression was not established, so this note makes no object-ID attribution for it. L12 p. 13 and R40 describe an unspecified family size `k` and then three plotted controls; A41 p. 12 instead displays 100 controls in its intermediate family. This is a revision difference, not evidence that either draft supplies the saved optimizer `U` rows.

The rendered L12 pages reproduce the already documented printed model/result conflict: [B04 restoration](b04-restoration.md) proves the displayed `x₀` and `B` cannot yield the printed endpoint or value. The first-action convention, full stage/control domains, exact generating project, unit authority and optimizer rows remain unresolved. No [historical scenario](../../benchmarks/scenarios/b04-historical.json), baseline value or canonical-run claim is changed by this inspection.
