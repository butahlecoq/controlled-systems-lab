## Contracts and measurable references

The [proof audit's construction matrix](reachability-proof-audit.md#construction-matrix) defines the following contracts. Here `R_t^lin` means all endpoints of complete feasible histories of the specified affine model, and `R_t^f` all endpoints of the *specified* nonlinear model under the same physical-coordinate initial, input and state constraints. The continuous-time reference must additionally specify hold functions and intersample constraints.

| Output | Reference | Established relation under stated exact assumptions | Additional evidence needed for a stronger claim |
|---|---|---|---|
| Full pairwise affine image, exact hull and stage intersection | `R_t^lin` | Exact equality by the L01 theorem plus the explicit clipping/feedback extension proved in the proof audit | Show that the historical implementation uses complete generators, exact intersection and the same inputs/coordinates. |
| Retained feasible candidate subset and exact hull, fed forward after clipping | `R_t^lin` | Inner, `H_t` subset `R_t^lin`; equality only with a sufficient complete discarded-point membership proof before clipping or another valid post-clipping proof | Resolve L05's actual retention predicate and identify its Table 1 generator; classify floating residuals separately. |
| Affine full or reduced set interpreted against the nonlinear model | `R_t^f` | Heuristic: neither inner nor outer inclusion is generally implied by linearization | A common-coordinate model, domain/remainder bounds, constraint-history coverage and propagated rounding/discretization bounds. |
| Finite nonlinear endpoints and plotted projections | `R_t^f` | Individual feasible endpoints are witnesses for their own histories; their convex hull need not consist of nonlinear endpoints | Exhaustive history coverage or a validated enclosure theorem, plus full-state rather than projected correspondence. |

For L05 B02, any set comparison first needs the same physical coordinates, initial states, control sets, horizon, stage constraints, and volume convention. Those fields cannot be inferred from vertex counts. Against named nonempty compact sets, report directed distances `delta(R,H)=sup_(r in R) inf_(h in H)||r-h||` and `delta(H,R)` separately. For compact convex sets, all-direction support gaps in the *dual* norm give Hausdorff distance; finitely sampled directions give sampled discrepancies unless a sufficient direction family or discretization bound is proved. For nonconvex `R_t^f`, support comparisons characterize its convex hull, not the exact endpoint set. These definitions and proofs are in the [metrics section](reachability-proof-audit.md#metrics-and-the-table-1-discrepancy).

## Evidence needed to resolve the remaining claim

1. Identify the versioned Table 1 generator and exact inputs for each row: source hash, driver, common-coordinate/reference-trajectory transform, horizon, initial/control/state sets, numerical tolerances, and saved run records. Map its actual retention predicate to a proof, including degenerate hulls and clipped feedback. L26/W12 do not supply this mapping.
2. If claiming an affine **inner** result, verify each returned generator is a feasible candidate and all subsequent hull and clipping steps preserve it. If claiming equality, provide complete omitted-point membership or another sound equality certificate. Distinguish a mathematical exact-arithmetic argument from a floating implementation check.
3. If claiming a nonlinear **outer** enclosure, bound the affine-to-nonlinear mismatch on a justified domain for every admissible history, include model/discretization/integration and roundoff contributions, propagate the tube, and handle state constraints and any geometric loss from inner reduction. The [conditional tracking lemma](reachability-proof-audit.md#conditional-model-error-lemma) states the required `eps_(j+1)=L_j eps_j+eta_j` hypotheses; no historical `L_j`, `eta_j` or domain certificate has been supplied.
4. If claiming a nonlinear **inner** set or two-sided Hausdorff error, prove realizability/coverage in the required direction. A finite sample, a smaller polytope volume, a numerical tolerance or an affine inner hull does not supply that proof.

This comparison narrows implementation correspondence: the inspected L26/W12 listing cannot substantiate the L05 Table 1 modified-method or nonlinear-enclosure claims. It does not resolve issue #4, disprove the intended historical method, or establish absence from every archive location.
