The new architecture ticket is a narrow shared admission path for fixed-plan propagation. Four representation-specific implementations repeat structural checks against `FixedPlanRequest`. A shared check can make those rules local and support cross-representation contract tests, while each implementation keeps its own mathematical admission, enclosure, approximation relation, and assurance behavior. ## Delegated decision and accepted ticket

### Localize shared fixed-plan request admission

**Strength:** Worth exploring. The delegated panel had two answers. Luna favored a focused ticket after the pilot. Terra favored deferral because the shared checks are short and preserving diagnostics matters. The coordinator chose the focused ticket, scheduled after #945. [Issue #951](https://github.com/butahlecoq/controlled-systems-lab/issues/951) is a child of #936 with native blocker #945. It adds no prerequisite to the pilot or to #943, #941, or #945.

`FixedPlanRequest` carries the linear system, initial set, fixed controls, disturbances, admissible state sets, and request identity ([header](../../include/csl/reachability/fixed_plan_disturbance.hpp#L10)). The box, ellipsoid, parallelotope, and zonotope propagators each inspect the horizon vectors and safe-set profile before propagation ([box](../../src/reachability/finite_plan_box.cpp#L90), [ellipsoid](../../src/reachability/finite_plan_ellipsoid.cpp#L162), [parallelotope](../../src/reachability/finite_plan_parallelotope.cpp#L229), [zonotope](../../src/reachability/finite_plan_zonotope.cpp#L238)). The shared horizon and state-dimension checks recur, but the algorithms also have real differences. Parallelotope propagation is limited to a four-state frame. Zonotope propagation has a generator cap. Ellipsoid and box traces have their own outward-enclosure calculations. Those checks must remain local.

**Deletion test:** Deleting the shared module would put the same structural checks back in four implementations. The shared request seam has multiple real adapters: `finite_plan_box` and `finite_plan_ellipsoid` are two; `finite_plan_parallelotope` and `finite_plan_zonotope` are additional implementations.

**Accepted ticket scope:** #951 inventories the common invariants and each adapter’s validation order, then consolidates only genuinely common structural admission. It preserves diagnostic fields and messages, mixed-invalid precedence, stop behavior, representation-specific limits, enclosure math, inclusion direction, and assurance. The ticket requires tests through at least two real adapters. It is scheduled after the #945 pilot.

**Existing issue comparison:** #943 owns reachable-set/MPC feedback composition across five representations. #944 owns wiring vertex LP/simplex, conversion, and optimizers into control pipelines. Neither issue owns this fixed-plan admission refactor. #943 has native dependencies #941 and #942; #944 has #942. #951 has native blocker #945 and parent #936. It does not block the pilot or its upstream tasks. The audit discussion is recorded in the [#943 comment](https://github.com/butahlecoq/controlled-systems-lab/issues/943#issuecomment-6788713354) and [#944 comment](https://github.com/butahlecoq/controlled-systems-lab/issues/944#issuecomment-6788713719).
