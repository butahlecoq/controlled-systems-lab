## 3. What the APP-01 admission currently proves, and what it does not

- `run_registered` admits an inner/outer result only through one of five structural bases: same immutable handle, empty subset, universal superset, exact stored-H row containment (inner: reference rows retained in output; outer: output rows retained in reference), or exact H/singleton point containment under checked dyadic arithmetic on stored binary64 values (`src/geometry/approximation/policy.cpp` lines 67–108; contract table approximation.md lines 20–28).
- Everything else fails closed: "Requested enclosure has no independently checkable inclusion evidence" (policy.cpp lines 169–170); a provider error bound is rejected because no independent bound verifier exists (lines 152–154); output must satisfy the primitive `size_budget` (lines 109–121, 160–162). No nontrivial box/parallelotope/zonotope/ellipsoid output is admitted today.
- Remaining APP-01 work is explicit: "A nontrivial box, parallelotope, zonotope or ellipsoid adapter needs an independent inclusion verifier with claim-specific evidence, a compatible output-size accounting method, and contract tests on analytic inner/outer cases. The planned APP-02/03/04 adapters must not reinterpret numerical residuals or provider declarations as inclusion proofs" (approximation.md lines 120–133).
- The objective-direction rule follows the nesting direction for the *same* objective (`objective_bound_side`, `include/csl/geometry/approximation.hpp` lines 22–26; policy.cpp lines 173–181; approximation.md lines 59–67), and inner reachable states need a verified trajectory before an achievable value is published (reachable_plan.cpp lines 164–183; approximation.md lines 69–74, 107–118).
- Measured evidence today is three literal scalar fixtures; the saved report states: "Binary64 supplied examples only; value 3 is conditional on original-problem verification. No validated inclusion, optimality, reachable-set completeness, comparator, speedup, or established performance limiter" (`reports/algorithm-results/app01-publications.json` line 4, measured scope line 5, source commit `22d406fd…` line 10).

## 6. The zonotope-intersection caveat and what it implies for comparisons

- Ordinary zonotopes are centrally symmetric; intersecting `[-1,1]^2` with `a+b≤0` yields a triangle, which is not centrally symmetric — so an arbitrary linear cross-step constraint leaves the zonotope class while remaining a convex polytope; the architecture recommends "a compatible polyhedral/CZ representation or an explicitly labeled approximation" ([temporal-coupling](temporal-coupling.md) lines 108–111). The same caveat heads the exact-operations table: "Zonotopes: exact affine images and Minkowski sums; intersections generally require another form or approximation" (modular line 174).
- GEO-07's closure enforces the label rule: unsupported intersections "do not become ordinary zonotopes by silent approximation" (GEO-07.md line 16; generator-sets.md lines 38–41; #32 closure comment). APP-03 acceptance requires that "Intersections advertise their actual resulting class" and that shared-factor dependencies be preserved or their relaxation recorded (APP-03.md line 16).
- For exact-versus-approximate comparisons this means: (a) each result must declare exact/equivalent/inner/outer/heuristic direction, assumptions and evidence (modular line 183; EXP-03.md line 16); (b) a reachability step that intersects with state constraints cannot assume ordinary-zonotope closure — it must route through a polyhedral/CZ form or carry an explicit enclosure label (temporal-coupling lines 108–111); (c) containment evidence, not volume or sampled support agreement, decides the inner/outer relation (EXP-03.md line 16; issue #4 body); and (d) objective bounds inherit the direction of the nesting — outer gives optimistic lower bounds, inner gives achievable upper bounds only with a recovered realizable trajectory (modular line 185; approximation.md lines 59–74).
