A matching field means only that field matches; it does not prove common authorship, chronology, solver behavior or a canonical run.

## Next source-matching gate

For each claimed canonical run, identify a hash-matched generator and saved arrays for `x0`, every `A/B/D`, action and disturbance set, stage constraints, initial-action convention, nonlinear model/time grid, objective implementation, full control history and terminal row. Compare those against the publication at its printed precision, retain any contradictory original row, and publish corrected references under separate IDs. Record source-release permission before publishing private code or data. Until those anchors exist, neither issue #1 nor issue #2 meets its restoration acceptance criteria.
