Reading the paired projections

The left panel plots oxidizer mass against fuel mass (x1–x2); the right plots oxidizer flow against fuel flow (x3–x4). Both panels show the same attempt and stages. Retained finite-action graph nodes and backward-viable nodes are shown over stages. Graph search coverage is incomplete; the points do not define continuous reachable-set boundaries. Purple marks are terminal-stage graph nodes, not a terminal set. The red trace is the selected affine-model plan; the blue dashed trace is the observed nonlinear replay. The soft objective defines no terminal set.
