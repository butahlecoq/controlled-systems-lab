Reading the paired projections

The left panel plots oxidizer mass against fuel mass (x1–x2); the right plots oxidizer flow against fuel flow (x3–x4). Both panels use the same saved attempt and stage scale. Amber regions are outer fixed-plan box enclosures, not the full reachable set. Very narrow bounds can visually coincide with the plan and replay; the saved numerical bounds remain available in the plotted data. The selected plan and observed nonlinear replay are drawn separately. The fuel objective is soft and defines no terminal set.
