Scope and limits

These are coverage results, not a performance ranking. There are no five alternating timing repetitions. A graph outcome marked complete means its configured runner completed; it does not imply globally complete search or global optimality.

The illustrative backward-graph figures report beam width 8 and search_coverage=incomplete. Their dots are retained finite-action states, not continuous reachable-set boundaries; no convex hull is drawn. The selected plan is optimal only over its retained graph, and plant replay is shown separately.
