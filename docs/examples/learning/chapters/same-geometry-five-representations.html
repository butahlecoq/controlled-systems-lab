At d=(1,0), Q support is 1 and the enclosing disk support is √2. At d=(1,1), both supports equal 2. Q⊂E⊂[-√2,√2]². The H/V polytope, zonotope, parallelotope, and box are exact here. The ellipsoid is an outer enclosure. Actual operation support depends on the inventory profile and request.
