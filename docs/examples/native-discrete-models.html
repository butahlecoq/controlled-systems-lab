35 discrete native model factories

The catalogue supplies native C++ A, B, G, x₀, optional nominal external input, coordinate metadata, source qualification, and model-specific time units. A model record does not choose a horizon, objective, admissible sets, observation contract, or strategy. Its equation is xₖ₊₁ = Axₖ + Buₖ + Gwₖ.

Follow model 21 through all 15 native MPC replans → · Learning-book lesson

All 35 supplied numerical records were checked against 25,769 audited matrix/initial entries, bit for bit. This is a transcription audit, not empirical calibration. Models 22–35 retain their synthetic/test qualifications. An identity G is a per-step increment channel unless its source establishes a physical interpretation. Hours, quarters, days, and seconds remain distinct in the native time metadata.
