What F, B and I mean here

F is a finite axis carrier enclosing all-control forward states of the affine predictor. B is the admitted physical region conditional on a fixed feasible future tail. It is an inner construction, not a global backward reachable set. Carrier constraints participate in B admission, so the recorded full-state identity is I = F ∩ B = B. We display that identity; we never intersect projected polygons. A 2D overlap does not prove full-state feasibility. Saved H projections use numerical support checks; ellipsoid outlines use 128 segments. The plotted-data JSON retains each projection method and its saved tolerance or segment count.

The affine candidate is a recovered feasible-tail local numerical policy prediction. The executed nonlinear plant can leave its prediction regions. These plots establish neither global policy optimality nor nonlinear enclosure or continuous-time safety. A soft objective reference is not a hard terminal target. Admissible state bounds, where finite in both plotted coordinates, are a separate layer.
